Evgenia — known as The Potty Doula — is a certified Elimination Communication and potty training coach through Go Diaper Free, the educational program founded by Andrea Olson. She helps families move away from disposable diapers earlier than the Western average by translating the "what and why" of EC into a practical approach that works for everyday family life.
